## Anatomical choices

**Twin Leader:** one rooted trunk with an exposed Y-shaped fork and two leading terminal flowers. The visible fork follows the supplied R29/R23 reports without asserting a genetic cause.

**Crested Cola:** a broadened, grooved ribbon stem and an irregular fused flowering ridge, guided by R16/R27 stem photographs and R30 flowering crest. A uniformly enlarged ordinary bud is not used as a substitute.

**Triple Whorl:** three separate leaf stalks originating at one stem node. The detail sheet includes numbered three-way node studies; these are three fan leaves, not just three leaflets on a leaf. Studied R06. A whorl is distinct from chromosome count.

**Pocket Dwarf:** visibly compressed internodes and branch reach, retained mature leaf and flower detail, and much shorter overall height above the same-sized pot. Studied R24 and R22, treating them as stature analogues rather than proof of a particular dwarf gene.

**Triploid:** plausible ordinary cannabis architecture, guided by the supplied R09 comparative plants and R10 chromosome/DNA-content figure. The copper 3x badge is a proposed game identity marker; appearance alone does not confirm triploidy. No mandatory giant buds, thick leaves, three-way nodes or crest are assigned as a diagnostic trait.

All green/form/plum alternatives are original art proposals; the reference images do not verify each host-color/trait combination or inheritance mechanism. Supplied source credits, provenance URLs, inspected reference IDs and output hashes are in review-manifest.json. Original source photographs are unmodified outside the repository; newly drawn concepts are not photo collages.
