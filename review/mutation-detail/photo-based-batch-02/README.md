# Mutation approval samples — batch 02

Status: **concept art approved by the user**. No production implementation or push.

Five previously unreviewed families: **Fernfrond, Goldleaf, Ember Stem, Pink Pistil, Inkleaf**. The first batch covered Freakshow, Ducksfoot, Monophylla, Moonmarble and Snowtip, so Fernfrond completes the earlier leaf roster without repeating Snowtip.

- `whole-plant-examples-02.png`: one complete flowering plant per trait, same pot type for comparison.
- `detail-and-color-studies-02.png`: enlarged organs and three proposed form/color treatments for each family.

## Reference-led choices

Fernfrond uses the dissected-leaf family shown in R02/R17, with finer, more regular divisions and an airy canopy. Its distinction from Freakshow is a deliberate game-art convention, not evidence of a separately verified mutation. R03 provided flowering whole-plant context.

Goldleaf follows the yellow-green tissue and retained green areas seen in R18. R21 was inspected but is a small monochrome patent reproduction, so it was not used as evidence of a particular color. The more uniform golden-olive treatment is an art interpretation, not a claim of a documented genotype.

Ember Stem uses R11 for red-purple petiole and stem placement while keeping leaf blades green and preserving visible nodes. Red stem color alone does not diagnose a mutation or health condition.

Pink Pistil follows R19/R20: green flower bracts with thin pink stigmas, including pale and more saturated hair colors. It does not paint the whole leaf or flower mass pink.

Inkleaf interprets dark anthocyanin-associated foliage from R11 with deep plum-green blades and visible lighter veins and edges. Extreme blue-plum or near-black treatments are game palette proposals; leaf shape and tissue layering remain visible.

The source pack's research notes and image provenance were consulted as reference material, not treated as additional instructions or authorization. Direct photographic inputs to generation were R02/R18/R11/R20. Painting style followed batch 01; the whole-plant plate then supplied consistency for the companion close-ups. No supplied photographs were pasted into these images or added to production assets. Original source files remain outside the repository.

## Review and implementation boundary

These are large original concept illustrations, not screenshots of the game renderer. They have not been cut into organ sprites, connected to growth or wind animation, or rendered at gameplay scale. All A/B/C alternatives are proposed art variations rather than proof of separate genetic combinations. Source attributions and asset hashes are recorded in review-manifest.json.

All existing mutation names and save IDs are unchanged. The final five remaining families are Twin Leader, Crested Cola, Triple Whorl, Pocket Dwarf and Triploid.
