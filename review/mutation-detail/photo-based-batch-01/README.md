## Sample families

- **Freakshow:** open, very narrow primary leaflets with uneven deep secondary cuts, curled lateral lobes and long tips; studied R17 and R02. Replaces the generic evenly repeated fern mask concept.
- **Ducksfoot:** connected tissue between lobes, coherent radiating veins, broad webbing and serrated outer margins; studied R05 and comparison R13.
- **Monophylla:** AD17 analogue with a single smooth-to-wavy, textured adult blade; studied R14 and whole-shoot R15. This corrects the earlier sharply serrated juvenile-style draft.
- **Moonmarble:** irregular pale tissue sectors and marbling across otherwise ordinary fan-leaf anatomy; studied R26, R28 and comparison R13. Photographs alone do not determine the cause or inheritance.
- **Snowtip:** partially pale upper growth with green supporting leaves. Studied the pale seedling R25 and pale sectors R26. A localized game treatment of overlapping chlorophyll-loss appearances, not proof of an independent natural mutation or a healthy all-white adult.

Each row has two green morphological/pattern variants and one proposed plum-green color combination. The third column is a game-art proposal, not evidence that every pictured combination occurs in a documented cultivar. No automatic inheritance or performance changes are implied.
