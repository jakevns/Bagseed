Art direction. Fields of Mistria and CrossCode inform individual facial features and readable expressions; Legend of Mana informs organic shapes and cloth; Eastward and Sea of Stars inform stance, warmth and personality; Roots of Pacha and Owlboy provide additional body and silhouette studies. The references informed original Bagseed artwork; their characters and assets are not imported into the game.

Before/after world views use the same measured game zoom and feet position. The new assets use a 96 × 108 raster, corresponding to a 32 × 36 world-space drawing canvas; the extra width accommodates hair, arm swing and Doug’s can. Their world feet coordinates and existing collision boxes are unchanged in the proposal. Integrating this detail needs a higher-density world render target and corresponding crop changes. No game implementation changes have been made.

The portrait pair under each front view is shown at the game’s 64-pixel dialogue size. The three expression studies are labeled enlargements. “Enlarged inspection” is not evidence of phone readability. Phone views are browser emulation, not a physical-device playtest.

Five identities, their standing views, generated four-frame walks and three portrait expressions are covered. Idle uses a subtle upper-body breathing transform over the standing assets. Blink variants, every customization option, all other residents, sitting, smell poses, flashlight attachment and copied-save integration remain future production work after art approval.

Character design references

Ten selected reference IDs across seven games, with source links. Screenshots, character illustrations and video frames are identified separately. Embedded DOCX images were inspected; external full-resolution hosts remained blocked.

Original character assets were created with image generation using the supplied reference artwork and the authored Bagseed identities. Source sheets, prompts, extracted cells, alignment measurements and preview code are retained. Reference artwork belongs to its respective creators; original-source links are provided. No game edit, deployment or push has been performed.
