Four exact source growth silhouettes per species, opaque basal anchors, root-fixed cached wind and reduced-motion stills. Main-pot drawing cancels the old rosette lift while preserving original soil coordinates and foreground rims. Native companion pots use the same artwork. Other species and named rare varieties keep their current renderer. No care, growth, eligibility, harvest, regrowth or bonus mechanics change.

`tools/compile-garden-plants.cjs` validates source/runtime hashes and approved status. `tools/build-bedroom.cjs` includes the adapter; the startup cover awaits all fifty atlases.
