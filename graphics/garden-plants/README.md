# Painted garden plants

Fifteen approved species: Lettuce P001, Bok choy P010, Basil P051, Mint P061, Marigold P098, Chamomile P070, Lavender P126, Calendula P151, Sweet alyssum P108 Nasturtium P105, Thyme P058, Chives P056, Dill P055, Cilantro P053 and Thai basil P052. Four exact source growth silhouettes per species, opaque basal anchors, root-fixed cached wind and reduced-motion stills. Main-pot drawing cancels the old rosette lift while preserving original soil coordinates and foreground rims. Native companion pots use the same artwork. Other species and named rare varieties keep their current renderer. No care, growth, eligibility, harvest, regrowth or bonus mechanics change.

`tools/compile-garden-plants.cjs` validates source/runtime hashes and approved status. `tools/build-bedroom.cjs` includes the adapter; the startup cover awaits all fifteen atlases. Approved source/media checkpoint: `review/garden-batch-01/` `review/garden-batch-02/` and `review/garden-batch-03/`. The batch plan is `review/garden-plant-plan/README.md`. Future drafts stay outside production until approved.

Lavender, Thyme and Chives store artwork uses their native starter-pot purchase form; other approved species use seed packets. Calendula stage 2/3 attachment points are inspected central stems, excluding lower hanging leaves.
