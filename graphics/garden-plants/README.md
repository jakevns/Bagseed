# Painted garden plants

Five approved species: Lettuce P001, Bok choy P010, Basil P051, Mint P061 and Marigold P098. Four exact source growth silhouettes per species, opaque basal anchors, root-fixed cached wind and reduced-motion stills. Main-pot drawing cancels the old rosette lift while preserving original soil coordinates and foreground rims. Native companion pots use the same artwork. Other species and named rare varieties keep their current renderer. No care, growth, eligibility, harvest, regrowth or bonus mechanics change.

`tools/compile-garden-plants.cjs` validates source/runtime hashes and approved status. `tools/build-bedroom.cjs` includes the adapter; the startup cover awaits all five atlases. Approved source/media checkpoint: `review/garden-batch-01/`. The batch plan is `review/garden-plant-plan/README.md`. Future drafts stay outside production until approved.
