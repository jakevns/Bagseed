# Painted garden plants

Forty approved species: Lettuce P001, Bok choy P010, Basil P051, Mint P061, Marigold P098, Chamomile P070, Lavender P126, Calendula P151, Sweet alyssum P108 Nasturtium P105, Thyme P058, Chives P056, Dill P055, Cilantro P053, Thai basil P052, Spinach P002, Swiss chard P004, Arugula P003, Rosemary P057 Lemon balm P062, Parsley P054, Sage P059, Oregano P060, Strawberry P071 Jalapeno P033, Kale P005, Mustard greens P007, Mizuna P008, Tatsoi P009 Collard greens P006, Tomato P031, Bell pepper P032, Carrot P011, Beet P012 Radish P013, Broccoli P021, Cauliflower P022, Cabbage P023, Kohlrabi P025 and Celery P027. Four exact source growth silhouettes per species, opaque basal anchors, root-fixed cached wind and reduced-motion stills. Main-pot drawing cancels the old rosette lift while preserving original soil coordinates and foreground rims. Native companion pots use the same artwork. Other species and named rare varieties keep their current renderer. No care, growth, eligibility, harvest, regrowth or bonus mechanics change.

`tools/compile-garden-plants.cjs` validates source/runtime hashes and approved status. `tools/build-bedroom.cjs` includes the adapter; the startup cover awaits all forty atlases. Lavender, Thyme and Chives store artwork uses their native starter-pot purchase form; other approved species use seed packets. Calendula stage 2/3 attachment points are inspected central stems, excluding lower hanging leaves.
