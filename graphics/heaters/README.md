# Heater artwork samples

Awaiting user artwork approval. Includes Old Space Heater ($24.99, 420W) and Thermo-Cube Digital Heater ($79, 330W), with matching store/inventory/delivery icons. Prices, purchases, installation, heater controls, failures, room heating, power calculations are unchanged. Bedroom heaters now sit beside the window watering station; grow-room anchors remain unchanged.

The unchanged generated PNG is retained in assets. Its fingerprinted runtime WebP decodes to identical RGBA pixels. The compiler verifies source/runtime/public hashes, crops and fixed aspect ratios; readiness is included in the startup gate.

Heater rendering follows native heating activity for the installed heater, power switch, thermostat, breaker, outage and heat-trip state. The digital target remains visible in powered standby, while coils and rising heat stop. LOW/HIGH affect warmth strength. A gentle coil pulse and three fine warm air trails animate only while heating. Reduced motion keeps a steady active coil with no moving trails. Nighttime coils and their amber floor wash use an emission-only pass after room shading, cut out by the actual alpha of foreground actors. The heater body stays in its ordinary depth layer. Click areas match the new silhouette and follow the bedroom window station. See ../room-climate/README.md for room placement and nearby controls.

Rebuild with `npm run build:bedroom`, verify with `npm run check`, capture the actual public/index.html with `node tools/preview-heaters.cjs`, then package its frames with `python3 tools/animate-heaters.py`. The preview checks actual final room pixels for both tiers: visible night coil/floor emission, unchanged daytime, zero powered-off emission and no light over opaque player pixels. It also checks both tiers in all four locations, manual/thermostat/off/trip/outage behavior, native room sharing, live targets, motion settings, night/day button lighting and store cards on desktop and phone. `node --test --test-concurrency=1 tests/heaters.test.cjs tests/starter-tent.test.cjs tests/tent-player-depth.test.cjs` checks real purchases, delivery, fitting, controls after reload, missing-art handling and existing tent/player layering.
