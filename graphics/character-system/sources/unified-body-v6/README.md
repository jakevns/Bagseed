## Preparation and repaint

`prepare-unified-body.py` reads the original paintings at native resolution and
the exact arm ownership used in column 1 of the previous Standing boards. It
removes those arm pixels and grows the hole mask by 6 native pixels. The user
subsequently explicitly approved adding leftover arm ink: nearby interior ink is
included, with base mask and added-pixel mask stored separately. All views are
padded onto 768x1024 canvases without scaling the original painting.

Six first attempts and four correction attempts used built-in imagegen with the
input and guide mask as references. The tool has no enforced mask parameter.
`restore-unified-body.py` enforces preservation afterward: it registers returned
paint with a single similarity transform based on unchanged features, copies
new pixels inside the revised mask, then pastes every original RGBA pixel outside
that mask back exactly. It asserts equality and records the registration. It
rejects degenerate feature fits (including zero scale); a framing-only fallback
is explicitly marked unverified. No bone-based body reshaping is used.

New skin is color-matched by channel gain to the surrounding 10px same-part skin
ring. This changes only new masked pixels, preserving paint texture. Average
matching does NOT prove a seamless patch or correct shoulder. Correction-input
PNGs are derived preparation products and are regenerated by the restoration
script; attempt PNGs and prompt text preserve the model results and requests.
The first six calls preceded the approved ink-mask extension; base-mask.png and
hole.png retain that original mask definition.

Female-left and male-left first outputs were blocked by the image service. The
female-right correction was also blocked; its first output remains the draft.
Blocked requests were not retried. 10/12 calls were used; two remain unspent.
The four available views still fail visual and/or numeric acceptance.

## Shared-source cuts and arm

`cut-unified-body.py` cuts only the single restored armless painting. Torso ends
10px below waist; hips begin 10px above waist and end 15px below buttock fold;
thigh begins 15px above fold and ends 12px below knee; calf+foot begins 12px above
knee. Boundaries are shallow 4px curves over actual painted cross-sections.
Overlap retains the same opaque skin pixels. No ink is painted on body cuts,
and no circles, rotated-copy caps, synthesized skin or new silhouettes are used.
Source joints come from the registered native joints in the existing contract's
source report. Buttock fold heights are explicitly annotated from the paintings.

Order: darkened far leg, calf+foot, thigh, hips, torso, near arm. Native upper arm
and forearm use curved 12px elbow/wrist overlaps. Only missing back-edge arm ink
is restored, using source ink color and 3px width. Approved hand source pixels
are resampled uniformly at their measured wrist, color-matched to the forearm,
and cut inside the wrist overlap; original approved PNGs are never overwritten.
Manual wrist registrations are recorded because v4 hands have different stub
lengths. These arm contours/registrations remain visually unapproved.

## Checks and images

Every available view has a four-panel PNG: armless source; assembled Standing
beside the untouched original; +25/-25-degree thigh tests; +20/-20-degree arm
tests. Rotation canvases are padded so nothing is cut off. Thigh tests move calf
and foot with the thigh. Every source part retains its native proportions.

`checks.json` and `audit.json` report exact outside-mask preservation, Standing
silhouette XOR/union (<2%), per-region mean BGR differences (<3%), hole/ring mean
color (<4%), dark exterior outline coverage (>=95%), native contour jumps near
joins (<=2px), and transparent pixels at sampled attachment interiors. Natural
background enclosed BETWEEN limbs is reported separately and is not called a
body hole. AA overlap can slightly alter the 128-alpha silhouette threshold;
the cut-reassembly difference is reported, never rounded to zero.

These checks do not establish visually smooth paint. Residual shoulder shading,
repair boundaries and arm contour artifacts still fail visual review. No passed
flat-patch, stray-outline or six-view approval is claimed. Blocked views have no
fabricated result. No walk integration is allowed until the user approves.
