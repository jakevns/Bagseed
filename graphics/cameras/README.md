# Camera artwork — approved

Three existing equipment tiers: Cracked Phone (included), Snapwell Compact ($89), Lumen DSLR ($349). The separate Vintage Camera Display decoration ($20) is included too. The DSLR retains its floor tripod, compact retains its desk corner, and a flat phone view uses that corner when the starter camera is equipped, provided no tabletop decoration overlaps it. Phone presentation adds no collision or new placement restriction.

Source atlas and technical crop/hash data are retained under assets and sources. Room, store, inventory and delivery cards share these sprites. Photo resolutions (72/120/240), angle/filter unlocks, photo rendering, saved equipment IDs and tripod collision remain unchanged. Cameras have no newly invented movement or flash loop.

The compiler is tools/compile-cameras.cjs; both build paths include it through tools/build-bedroom.cjs. tools/preview-cameras.cjs loads public/index.html and validates the integrated artwork. The user approved the picture board and requested publication with “Send it!!”.
