# Bog Lantern — Lavender Marsh connected component prototype

Status: artwork samples awaiting approval. This batch contains the final named catalog strain plus two explicitly requested experimental phenotype options. Full native painted-renderer replacement remains pending.

Native lineage: ??? × Marsh Light; additional Bog Lantern experimental phenotype. Artwork supplies broad sage-blue fans, airy lilac flowers and peach pistils, with lavender finishing leaves applied in the shared renderer. Healthy fan source sprites stay green. Lavender Marsh is a review phenotype label, not a new native strain. `lineId` stays `boglantern`. Its broad / blue / Lavender expression differs from the earlier narrow / forest / Red accents kit. The `boglantern@1` default and every earlier saved identity remain unchanged.

Sixteen isolated organs cover seed, radicle, cotyledons, single/three/five/seven-leaflet fans, sugar leaf, stem, Y junction, early/mid/late female flowers, closed/open male flowers and bamboo support. Crop bounds, attachment anchors, alpha hulls and SHA256 hashes are measured in the manifest. Source PNG pixels are preserved; WebP encoding is verified for exact RGBA equivalence. Nonoverlapping source crops are checked, and repeat compilation may not rewrite an existing part ID.

`boglantern-lavender@1` registers 15 integration-draft roles. The shared connected model handles expanding secondary branches, stable IDs, exact painted height, fixed-root wind, reduced motion, graded branch/popcorn buds and persisted canopy cleanup with larger retained tops. Senescence, stress, supports and care remain separate from genetic selection. The existing cool-night system deepens this receptive phenotype's color after an actual 10°F lights-off differential; 8°F earns no qualifying exposure. No native genetics, growth timing, climate or Sprout behavior is changed.

Variant registration uses `lineId` and `registerAsDefault: false`. The compiler stores an additional profile without adding a fake entry to `library.strains` or changing the earlier default. Experimental organs constrain leaf shape, leaf palette and flower palette. Select this compatible review family explicitly with `createIdentity(plant, expressedTraits, library, {profile: "boglantern-lavender@1"})`; once saved, its identity stays pinned through save/reload and clones. Incompatible expressed traits are rejected rather than silently recolored or genetically rerolled. This does not yet install a native automatic phenotype-to-profile adapter.

Samples in `review/boglantern-lavender-components/` include seed through harvest-ready, late-flower senescence, 50/90-inch physical scale, natural/cool-night colors, and 120 frames / 10 seconds of growth and wind. The controlled height studies do not override native growth or autoflower timing. Full care/training, diagnostic-state, drying-organ and camera/depth integration remain pending.

Reproduce: `python3 tools/compile-plant-components.py boglantern-lavender`, `node tools/preview-plant-components.cjs boglantern-lavender`, `python3 tools/verify-plant-wind.py boglantern-lavender`.
