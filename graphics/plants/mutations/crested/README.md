# Crested Cola components

Status: **user-approved gameplay implementation for production**.

Scalloped-fan and curled-comb forms use a painted grooved ribbon stem and separate early, mid and late fused female flowering crests. The terminal flower is a new broad organ, not a horizontally stretched ordinary bud. Lateral and popcorn buds remain normal, graded and attached. The flower shader applies the inherited host palette, eligible cool-night pigment and Pink Pistil stigma layer. Male plants retain native male flower organs on the flattened stem.

The structural layer is independent of leaf shape, vascular/variegated tissue, chlorophyll and female-stigma layers. The original strain leaf and flower kit identities remain pinned in the saved visual recipe. Both forms derive deterministically from the existing saved art seed and expressed foliage; clones retain the form. No mutation genes, inheritance probabilities, yield/speed/mold statistics or catalog entries were changed. The existing growth, supports, wind, stress and canopy cleanup systems remain in use.

Original generated PNGs live under `assets/`; the compiler registers attachment geometry and losslessly encodes RGBA-exact WebP with SHA-256 checks. Published atlases live under `public/assets/plants/mutations/crested/`. The normal bedroom builder retains the new `public/assets/embedded/` image pipeline. Do not edit generated HTML or re-embed image data by hand. All three new atlases participate in startup readiness.

Study references: supplied `Bagseed_Mutation_References.zip`, R16/R27 (flattened stem photographs); R30 (flowering context, not proof of a specific cause). Approved concept: `review/mutation-detail/photo-based-batch-03/structure-and-color-studies-03.png`. Photo-based morphology guides the art; it is not a diagnosis of a particular genetic cause. Source photos remain outside the repository.
