Pots keep their soil anchors and native +10 floor depth. A rear/soil pass draws before each growing plant and a masked near rim/body afterward, so the stem sits inside the opening without showing through the bag. The front mask removes the upper/back half as well as the soil opening, keeping the rear rim and probe behind the plant. These masks are generated at runtime from source-relative soil ellipses; the source art is never altered. Empty pots and drying stumps remain supported. Soil shade and smart probe percentage follow actual moisture. The probe has a distinct red LED below 30% moisture and green at 30% and above, with a small jewel highlight and a localized halo that strengthens at night. The LED emission is composed after room tint so red and green keep their brightness at night. Later plant, tent and foreground actor illustrations cut out the emission using their actual alpha and transforms; the pot body is never repainted over them. The display and button retain restrained night halos. Camera feeds use the same rear/front pot layers; remote views remain read-only.
